Privacy and analytics

Digital Atom uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page views and scrolling. The Analytics tag stays off until you choose "Allow analytics" on the home page.

If you allow Analytics, the site also records a generic event when someone clicks the email link. That event records the contact method, not the email address. Advertising signals are disabled.

Your choice is stored in this browser. You can change your Analytics choice at any time.

Google provides the Analytics service. Read Google's Privacy Policy. For questions about this site, email hello@digitalatom.ru.
